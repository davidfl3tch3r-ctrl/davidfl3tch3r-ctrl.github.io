Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

The Armband: FPL Assistant ("the app") is operated by David Fletcher ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what we do not do. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version. The app has no user accounts. We store your public Fantasy Premier League team ID and the recommendations we work out from it. We do not ask for your name, your address or your date of birth. We never see your Fantasy Premier League password. There is no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold to anyone.

What we collect

  • Your FPL team ID. This is a public number that identifies a Fantasy Premier League entry. You either type it in yourself or the app reads it after you sign in to Fantasy Premier League. We use it to fetch your squad from the public Fantasy Premier League API and to generate advice.
  • Squad and gameweek data derived from that ID. Your players, your bank, your chips, your transfer history and your league standings, as published by Fantasy Premier League. We cache the resulting recommendations so the app opens quickly.
  • A device token, only if you turn on notifications. Used to send deadline reminders. You can turn this off in the app at any time, or in iOS Settings, and the token is deleted or disabled.
  • Purchase status. If you subscribe, our payments provider (RevenueCat, working on top of Apple's App Store) tells us whether an anonymous app user identifier has an active subscription. We do not receive your card details, and Apple does not share them with us.
  • Basic technical logs. Server request logs including IP address, kept for a short period for security and debugging.

Signing in to Fantasy Premier League

The app offers an optional "Sign in with FPL" step. When you use it, the official Fantasy Premier League website is loaded inside the app and you sign in there, on Premier League's own page. Your email address and password are typed into Premier League's form and are sent to Premier League. We do not see them, we do not transmit them anywhere, and we do not store them. What the app takes from that session is your team ID and the squad data the Fantasy Premier League site returns to it. You can skip this entirely and type your team ID instead.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, postal address or phone number.
  • No Fantasy Premier League password or session credentials.
  • No payment card details.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data or precise location.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because there is nothing to track.

How the data is used

Only to make the app work: fetching your squad, producing recommendations, remembering your preferences, sending the deadline reminders you asked for, and confirming whether your subscription is active. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell, rent or share your data with data brokers.

Where it is stored

Data is held on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the European Union, and with RevenueCat for subscription status. Some of your settings, including your linked teams and your chosen style, are stored only on your own device.

How long we keep it

Cached recommendations are overwritten each gameweek. A linked team ID is kept while the app is in use. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. If you ask us to delete your data we will remove the stored team ID and any cached recommendations tied to it.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of the data we hold about your team ID, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email thearmbandgame@gmail.com with the team ID in question and we will action it within 30 days. You can also remove a team inside the app, and deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. It contains no user generated content, no chat and no social features.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted in the app.
