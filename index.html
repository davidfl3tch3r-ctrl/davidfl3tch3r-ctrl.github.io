Your whole gameweek, decided in one screen.

The Armband reads your Fantasy Premier League squad and tells you who to captain, which transfer actually gains you points, and the right week to play your Bench Boost or Triple Captain.

Get it on the App Store

What it does

  • A ranked captain call with the reasoning behind it, not just a name.
  • Transfers scored on projected gain, with injuries and rotation risk flagged before they cost you.
  • Chip timing: the gameweeks where Bench Boost and Triple Captain are actually worth it.
  • A scout report on the players rising and falling before the crowd notices.
  • A build mode where you can tinker freely and see the exact plan that comes out.

Free and Premium

The Armband is free to use. Armband Premium is a single annual subscription that unlocks multiple teams, the full transfer planner and the detailed chip strategy. There is no advertising and nothing is sold on.
