Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Restored: Recovery Score ("the app") is made by David Fletcher ("we", "us"). It calculates a daily recovery score from the health data already on your iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what the app reads, what it does with it, and what it does not do.

The short version. Everything happens on your device. The app reads your Health data with your permission, works out a score, and shows it to you. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We cannot see your data because it never leaves your phone or watch.

Health data the app reads

With your explicit permission through Apple Health, the app reads the following categories, on the device only:

  • Heart rate variability (SDNN) recorded overnight by Apple Watch.
  • Resting heart rate.
  • Respiratory rate recorded during sleep.
  • Sleep analysis (time asleep, time in bed, sleep stages).
  • Active energy and workouts from the previous day, used as a measure of strain.

Each reading is compared with your own recent history to produce a 0–100 recovery score. The app does not write anything to Apple Health. You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Restored, and the app will simply compute with whatever is still available.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app has no backend. Health data is read from Apple Health, processed in memory on your device, and displayed. It is not stored by us, transmitted to us, or shared with any third party. The app does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than showing you your own recovery score, and it never sells or discloses that data, in line with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time unlock, handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no card details and no personal information about you. Purchase status is checked on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or advertising identifiers. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Apple Watch

The Watch app reads the same Health categories directly on the watch to show your score there. Like the iPhone app, it keeps everything on the device.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored locally (your first-launch date and purchase state). Your Health data itself stays in Apple Health under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

The recovery score is a wellness indicator derived from consumer wearable data. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition, and should not be used to make medical decisions. If you feel unwell, speak to a doctor.
