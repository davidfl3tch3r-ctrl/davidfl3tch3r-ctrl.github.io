Support

Something not working? Email thearmbandgame@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a person, usually within a day.

How do I connect my team?

On first launch, either sign in with your Fantasy Premier League account or tap "Enter my team ID instead" and type the number. Your team ID is the number in your FPL points link, for example fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/2366206/event/1.

Where does the advice come from?

Public Fantasy Premier League data: form, fixtures, expected goals and assists, minutes, price moves, ownership and injury status. The app projects points for each player over the coming gameweeks and compares your squad against the alternatives you can actually afford.

Do I need to give you my FPL password?

No. Signing in is optional and, if you do it, it happens on the official Premier League website loaded inside the app. We never see your password. You can always use the team ID route instead.

What does Premium include?

Multiple teams, the full transfer planner across more than one move, and the detailed chip strategy view that tells you which gameweek to use Bench Boost and Triple Captain. The free tier stays fully usable.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then The Armband, then Cancel Subscription. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I restored my phone and lost Premium

Open the You tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Notifications are not arriving

Check the toggle in the You tab, then check iOS Settings, Notifications, The Armband, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

Delete my data

Remove the team in the app, or email thearmbandgame@gmail.com with your team ID and we will delete the stored record.
